About MAGEDE Beach Resort

Welcome to MAGEDE Beach Resort, where luxury meets the natural beauty of Cape Town's stunning coastline. Since our establishment, we have been dedicated to providing our guests with an unforgettable beachfront experience that combines world-class hospitality with the relaxed atmosphere of a coastal paradise.

Our Story

Founded with a vision to create a haven where guests can escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life, MAGEDE Beach Resort has become one of Table View's premier destinations. Our name reflects our commitment to creating magical experiences for every visitor who walks through our doors.

Nestled along the pristine shores of Table View, our resort offers breathtaking views of Table Mountain and the Atlantic Ocean. We pride ourselves on our attention to detail, exceptional service, and commitment to sustainability.

Our Mission

At MAGEDE Beach Resort, our mission is simple: to provide every guest with an exceptional experience that exceeds expectations. We strive to create a warm, welcoming environment where families, couples, and solo travelers alike can relax, rejuvenate, and create lasting memories.

What Sets Us Apart

Our dedicated team of hospitality professionals works tirelessly to ensure every aspect of your stay is perfect. From our elegantly appointed rooms to our gourmet dining options, from our rejuvenating spa treatments to our exciting water sports activities, every detail is carefully curated to provide you with the ultimate beach resort experience.

We believe in sustainable tourism and work closely with local communities to preserve the natural beauty of our coastline while providing meaningful employment opportunities and supporting local businesses.

Visit Us

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a solo adventure, MAGEDE Beach Resort is your perfect destination. Come experience the magic of Cape Town's coastline with us, and discover why our guests return year after year.
