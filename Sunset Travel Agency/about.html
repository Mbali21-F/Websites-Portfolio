About Sunrise Travel Agency

Founded in 2010, Sunrise Travel is a professional agency that crafts personalized travel experiences. Our team works with global partners to ensure each trip is unique, safe, and unforgettable. There is so much to see just outside your door. Whether you’re planning a summer vacation with the kids or a weekend getaway with your special someone, your next trip never felt so close.

Sunrise, the home of holiday packages, is South Africa’s most loved and highly rated leisure travel agency. Here at Sunrise we pride ourselves on delivering you the very best in customer experience and value on package holidays.

Are you dreaming of the pristine beaches of Mauritius, or cruising the oceans and scenic rivers of Europe? Or do you see yourself skiing down powdery mountain slopes? Is it a local getaway, exploring the rice fields of Thailand, or are you excited about a relaxing bush break? Perhaps you are a sports fan following your favourite team overseas or are you planning a group trip or an incentive travel experience as a reward.

We offer assistance for family holidays, honeymoon or anniversary packages, youth and student travel. We have beautiful coach touring itineraries, Disney breaks, city stays and so much more across the world. From Summer holidays to Winter breaks or anywhere in between, we have a dream holiday for it all. Start planning your 2026 holiday package today!
